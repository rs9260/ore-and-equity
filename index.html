ORE & EQUITY

Buy low, mine deep!

How to play

You have 10 days (about 15 minutes) to grow your net worth.

  • Mine coal, iron, gold and diamonds. Deeper ore is rarer and worth more.
  • Sell ore to the Merchant at the Trading Post. Prices move every day, and dumping lots of ore pushes them down.
  • Invest in village shares with each village's Banker. Shares pay dividends at dawn.
  • Deliver ore to a village's Quartermaster to boost its health and share price.
  • Borrow from the Trade Port bank to buy more, but interest is due every dawn.
  • Watch out for Murkmoles and flying Skimmers, which multiply as you get rich. Swords, armor and lanterns keep you safe. Let your swing recover between hits for full damage.
  • Your world saves automatically every 30 seconds and when you pause or quit, so pick it from the world list to carry on.
  • Buy the Golden Parachute wings from the Merchant, then press Space in mid-air to glide.
